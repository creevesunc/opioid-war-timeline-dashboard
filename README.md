# Opioid War-Era Timeline Dashboard

Interactive dashboard: the rise and fall of opium, heroin, and pharmaceutical painkillers in the United States during the Afghanistan War era (1999-2023).

## Sections
1. The Full Story - narrative overview
2. Afghan Supply - poppy cultivation & opium production
3. Rise & Fall - all opioids on one timeline (includes fentanyl for comparison)
4. Pill Flood - DEA ARCOS oxycodone/hydrocodone distribution 2006-2014
5. Pharma Revenue - OxyContin & Opana sales
6. The Makers - companies, products, legal findings
7. Heroin Deaths - CDC/NCHS T40.1
8. Money - heroin retail market & national cost
9. Incarceration - BJS drug-offense prisoners
10. Birth Cohorts - heroin deaths by birth year 1985-2010
11. Afghanistan's Role - what it did and didn't supply
12. Timeline Compare - milestone events vs. death curves
13. Chart Index - every chart & source

## Data sources
CDC/NCHS National Vital Statistics System | UNODC Afghanistan Opium Surveys | DEA ARCOS | GAO | SEC | DOJ | FDA | RAND (ONDCP) | BJS | White House CEA | U.S. Congress JEC
